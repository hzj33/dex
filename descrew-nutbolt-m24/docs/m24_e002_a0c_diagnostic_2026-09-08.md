## One demonstrated problem and its sole behavioral correction

The unchanged M24 URDF specifies `nut_joint axis="0 0 -1"`, with zero joint
origin RPY and an untilted identity object root. Positive nut DOF travel is
therefore physical -Z. The inherited `rotation_axis='+z'` made the original
E002 Cartesian tangent and torque projection inconsistent with its positive
DOF-angle criterion. Baseline samples explicitly record `[0,0,1]`.

The only behavior-changing edit is the E002-only `rotation_axis: '-z'`
override in `configs/task/XHandPasiniM24NutBoltE002.yaml`. Corrected samples
record `[0,0,-1]`; the torque/force sign conventions are now separable. Force
sign remains uncalibrated, both signs remain tested, and no gate, force limit,
friction, reward weight, initial pose, action amplitude or duration was changed.
The historical M24/E001 task retains its original configuration.

The correction does not solve pair drive, and neither JSON permits PPO.
