## Demonstrated conflict and single correction

Let `n=clip(new_high_water/delta_ref,0,1)`, `g=pair_gate`, and
`c=min(index_contact_confidence,thumb_contact_confidence)`.

| Component | Previous | This correction |
|---|---|---|
| Forward reward | `4*g*n` | unchanged |
| Invalid forward cost | `4*(1-g)*n` | `4*(1-c)*n` |
| Net of these two terms | `4*n*(2*g-1)` | `4*n*(g+c-1)` |

Previously even a forward step with two physical contacts was net-negative
when `g<0.5`. The positive reward already discounts torque quality, and strict
valid progress/success already require the original pair gate. Charging the
same deficit again suppresses the route toward higher-quality motion. The
normal-only torque source also cannot establish absence of friction torque.

The correction changes only `invalid_forward_source` to `contact_quality` in
E002. Legacy absent-config behavior stays `pair_quality`. A missing opposing
contact still incurs the same maximum cost. Contact alone with `g=0` earns no
positive motion reward; static/revisited high water earns none; reverse travel
retains its old penalty. Contact/torque thresholds, gate 0.50, all coefficients,
strict valid-progress, milestones and 30-degree success gates are unchanged.

This removes one adverse reward interaction. It cannot establish that the
remaining normal-only torque signal is sufficient or that five minutes can
learn the required stroke.
